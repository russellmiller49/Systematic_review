## Recording details that matter

- **Queue membership:** Show the saved pool name and member PICO badges from the actual guideline. An Owner/Admin configures membership in guideline **Settings → Combined abstract screening**; selecting a queue does not change membership.
- **Reviewer targets:** Reviewer quotas are the default open-queue workflow when configured. The target is a number of reviews, not a reservation of particular abstracts. Fixed assignments are an advanced option.
- **Navigation:** In the combined pool, show **Available**, **My reviewed**, search, selection, and Next. Owner/Admin also has **All abstracts**. In an individual queue, the filter names differ; record the controls visible in that queue.
- **Decisions:** Include, Exclude, and Maybe exist in both queues. Exclude needs an active reason. The `i`, `e`, `m`, `n`, `j`/`k`, arrow, and `1`–`9` shortcuts can be shown briefly if useful. Pooled decisions operate one logical abstract at a time.
- **Limits:** Keyword highlighting, AI prescreening, and batch exclusion belong to individual screening; do not show them as pooled features. If AI appears in PICO 1 footage, label it as optional decision support and keep the human vote explicit.
- **Blinding and finality:** Show only the reviewer's own decision and aggregate review count. Do not display a co-reviewer's unresolved choice in the reviewer cut.
- **Full text:** End at the title/abstract handoff. Full-text retrieval and exclusion have their own workflow and should be a separate tutorial.
